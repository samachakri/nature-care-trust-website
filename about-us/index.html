Our Sacred Calling

Rooted in Soil, Bound by Care.

Founded in 2006, Nature Care Trust bridges two profound human responsibilities: restoring sacred Indian forest biodiversity and providing dignified, therapeutic natural sanctuaries for our elders.

The Two Trees of Our Foundation

Eighteen years ago, amidst rapidly encroaching deforestation across the Sahyadri ranges of the Western Ghats, a group of botanists, geriatric physicians, and rural elders united with a singular vision.

We witnessed sacred groves—centuries-old pockets of protected virgin woodland—being fragmented. Simultaneously, we saw an epidemic of urban isolation affecting older citizens, separated from the healing rhythms of nature.

Nature Care Trust was established as a Public Charitable Trust to bind these two needs: healing the forest through native tree conservation, while offering our elders sanctuary, purpose, and community in the grove.

I

Native Canopy Restoration

We strictly propagate endemic Western Ghats trees—Banyan, Peepal, Jamun, Kadamba, and Arjun. No monoculture or invasive eucalyptus.

II

Elder Dignity & Respite

Barrier-free pathways, sensory ayurvedic herb gardens, and shaded gazebos provide seniors with free mental, physical, and emotional respite.

III

Complete Transparency

Every tree dedicated is geo-tagged. Donors receive transparent annual audit reports, 80G tax exemptions, and open visitor invitations.

Statutory & Legal Governance

Nature Care Trust operates with the highest standards of financial, ethical, and statutory governance.
