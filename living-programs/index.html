Ecology & Elder Dignity In Action

Our Living Programs

Four focused, high-impact conservation and wellness pillars engineered to replenish ecosystems, recharge ground aquifers, and honor senior citizens.

Pillar 01 • Watershed Conservation

Village Native Tree Plantation Drives

Across the Sahyadri foothills, rural agricultural ecosystems suffer severe water table depletion and topsoil erosion during harsh dry summers. Our flagship village program addresses this through native micro-watershed regeneration.

  • ✓ Community Sapling Nurseries: Managed by rural women self-help groups, cultivating over 150,000 native saplings annually.
  • ✓ Aquifer Recharge: Deep-rooting Ficus, Jamun, and Mahua trees restore underground aquifers and seasonal streams.
  • ✓ 3-Year Survival Stewardship: 88%+ 3-year survival rate monitored through GPS field audits.

Pillar 02 • Urban Reforestation

Apartment & Gated Community Greening

Urban concrete expands rapidly, depriving elderly residents of greenspace and fresh oxygen. We collaborate with resident welfare associations (RWAs) and residential societies to install high-density native bioswales and micro-forests.

  • ✓ Miyawaki Micro-Forests: 30x denser and 10x faster growing urban groves planted in unused perimeter corners.
  • ✓ Senior Organic Herb Plots: Raised beds of Tulsi, Brahmi, Ashwagandha, and Lemongrass tended by society elders.
  • ✓ Bird & Pollinator Corridors: Nectar-rich native shrubs bringing butterflies and sunbirds back to apartment complexes.

Pillar 03 • Geriatric Wellness

Elder Nature Respite & Day Retreats

Our sacred grove sanctuary is fully barrier-free. Designed in consultation with geriatric healthcare providers, the sanctuary offers peaceful day retreats for senior citizens, dementia care groups, and retired couples.

  • ✓ Zero-Incline Mulch Walkways: Gentle wood-chip trails easy on arthritic knees, alongside paved wheelchair ramps.
  • ✓ Sensory Woodland Therapy: Gentle tactile and olfactory engagement with aromatic resin trees and medicinal groves.
  • ✓ Intergenerational Days: Regular school sessions where youth read with elders beneath the shade of 100-year-old banyans.
