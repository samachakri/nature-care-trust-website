🌿 OUR CORE SERVICES & ACTIVITIES

Tree Plantation, Landscape Gardening & Nature Care

Rural Care

Village Tree Plantation

Large-scale native sapling plantation drives. Banyan, Peepal, Jamun across rural pathways, schools, farmlands, and community commons to enrich soil & groundwater.

Planting for Ecological Growth • 100+ 2.5 Lakh planted ↗
Gated Communities

Apartment & Office Landscaping

Balcony gardens, terrace greenery, corporate indoor landscaping, vertical living walls, and scheduled organic plant care for modern residential and commercial offices.

Custom green designs • Professional maintenance teams ↗
Biodiversity & Herbs

Real Herbal Nursery & Organic Saplings

Cultivation of authentic medicinal plants (Tulsi, Ashwagandha, Lemongrass, Amla) along with Miyawaki mini-forest development educating caretakers and botanical curiosity.

Heirloom native saplings • Organic compost & soil blend ↗
PURPOSE & ECOLOGY

A Small Sapling Today. A Greener World Tomorrow.

Trees support life, improve our surroundings, provide shelter for birds and wildlife, and help maintain ecological balance. Planting a tree is only the beginning. Protecting and nurturing it is equally important.

🍃

Cleaner Surroundings

Natural filters that absorb carbon, mitigate dust, and purify the air we breathe every day.

🦋

Protecting Biodiversity

Flourishing micro-ecosystems that provide habitats for birds, pollinators, and indigenous wildlife.

🌱

A Greener Future

Recharging groundwater reserves and leaving a greener footprint and heritage for upcoming generations.
